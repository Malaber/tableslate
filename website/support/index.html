Support

Keep the game moving.

Common answers for scores, recovery, appearance, and community definitions.

Where is my active game?

Open the Play tab and choose Resume. TableSlate saves every meaningful score change immediately, including before the app closes or the device restarts.

Can I correct an earlier Wizard round?

Open the active Wizard game, choose “View and edit previous rounds,” select the round, and tap the corrected bid or trick value. Totals update automatically.

How do I change light or dark mode?

Open Settings from the gear button, then choose System, Light, or Dark. System follows the current device appearance.

Why was my definition rejected?

Imports must use schema version 1, a lowercase hyphenated identifier, supported renderer and session values, bounded inputs and expressions, and valid JSON. Built-in identifiers cannot be replaced, and an imported update needs a higher definition version.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the TableSlate GitHub repository. Include the app version, device model, iOS version, and the smallest set of steps that reproduces the issue. Do not attach private score history unless you intend to share it.
