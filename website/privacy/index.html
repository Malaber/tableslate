Privacy

Your game night stays yours.

Effective 13 September 2026

The short version

TableSlate has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or backend. It does not collect or upload your players, scores, game history, or imported definitions.

Data stored on your device

TableSlate stores the player names, favorites, recent games, active score sheets, completed score sheets, settings, and definitions you import. This data is used only to provide the app’s features and remains in the app’s local storage.

Imported definitions

When you choose a local game-definition file, TableSlate reads and validates that file on your device. The app does not upload it or follow behavior-controlling network links from it.

Backups and deletion

Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your Apple settings. Deleting TableSlate removes its local app container. You can delete imported definitions inside the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page. If a future release adds online services, this policy and Apple privacy disclosures will be updated before those services launch.
